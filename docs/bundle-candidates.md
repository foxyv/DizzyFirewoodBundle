# Bundle candidates

Items in Sailwind that Dizzy Bundle Up might bundle, stack, bag or hang. A checked box means the mod already handles it.

The number is the item's prefab ID in the game. Food comes from the game's own food script (`ShipItemFood`). Slices are the pieces a knife cuts off whole food.

## Already bundled (not food)

- [x] Firewood (`108` is the crate): tied bundles, G from crates and the ground
- [x] Candles (`131` is the crate): ribbon bundles that load lanterns
- [x] Fishing hooks (`104` is the crate): strung on a line that hangs from a lamp hook

## Non-food candidates

### Strong candidates

- [ ] Bottles: 40 empty bottle, 55 water bottle, 56 coco wine, 57 honey beer, 58 rice beer, 59 wine. A rope-tied cluster or carry crate of 6, perhaps hung by the necks from a lamp hook.
- [ ] Tobacco packs (320–324) and loose tobacco (310, 312, 314, 316, 318). Packs stacked like a sausage tower and tied with twine. Colors don't mix.
- [ ] Mugs: 100 wood, 101 clay, 102 metal, 103 gold. A stacked column, or hung by their handles from a lamp hook.
- [ ] Lantern oil bottles (132). A tied bundle that feeds lanterns one at a time, like candles.
- [ ] Elixirs: 96 energy, 97 sleep, 98 random. A small tied cluster or a net bag.

### Good fits for existing shapes

- [ ] Maps and scrolls (91, 115–119, 139, 165, 380). Rolled and tied with a colored ribbon to label them.
- [ ] Fishing rods (95). A long tied bundle like firewood.
- [ ] Oakum (166). Tied hanks for hull repairs.
- [ ] Tea and coffee boxes (373, 387–389). Stacked and tied with cord.

### Possible but less useful

- [ ] Pots (156, 157). Nested inside one another, or hung from a hook.
- [ ] Spyglasses (160–162), slicing knives (370–372) and the hammer (159). A tool roll.
- [ ] Oars (168). A lashed pair.

Furniture, beds, sails, stoves, racks, paintings, clocks and compasses are placed once rather than handled in numbers. Cargo crates and barrels (1–34, 201–234) are already containers.

## Notes

- **Fish letters:** A, E and M are probably the regions (Aestrin, Emerald, Medi). That's a guess from the names.
- **Firefish:** there's a firefish slice (365) but no whole firefish among the numbered prefabs.
- **Not in the food lists:** soups, drinks and bottles use different scripts. Bottles are under the non-food candidates instead.
- **Fuel wood:** `71 fuel wood` is most likely the firewood log the mod already bundles, since the game calls it "firewood".
